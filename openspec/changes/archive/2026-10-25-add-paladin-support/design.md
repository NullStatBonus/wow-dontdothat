## Context

Class support is data-driven: `Rules.Load` takes a class table, `Context.Profile` finds it by the player's class, and trainer, filter, ledger and options pages follow. Only the spell tables (`Data/SpellData.lua`, generated by `tools/build-spell-data.py`) and the milestone targets (`Model/Milestones.lua`, one global table read by `Options.lua`) are class-specific in practice. `PlayerProfile.Proficiencies` has only Hunter but nothing reads it, so it needs no Paladin entry.

Paladin facts below come from the game's own tables on wago.tools: WoW Forever build 1.60.1.70532 and Classic Era build 1.15.9.70003 (`SkillLineAbility` with class mask 2, `Talent`, `TalentTab`). What a Paladin trainer actually sells, and its prices, are not in those tables and stay UNVERIFIED until captured with `/ddt export`.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** every Paladin spell family in either build is classified on purpose; a per-class milestone override and note; the joke.

**Non-Goals:** weapon and mana-water advice, profession wording, rank skips without a source, the Warlock case, Season of Discovery rune abilities (see decision 4).

## Decisions

1. **`Paladin.lua` mirrors `Hunter.lua`.** Specs Holy (tree 1), Protection (tree 2), Retribution (tree 3). Tab order is verified: `TalentTab.OrderIndex` is 0, 1, 2 for Holy, Protection, Retribution in both builds, and the talent trees are identical in both. Rotations carry `needsReview = true` because they are judgement from the class brief, not a sourced guide.
2. **Talent spells (verified from `Talent`).** Nine active spells come from talents: Consecration, Divine Favor and Holy Shock (Holy); Blessing of Kings, Blessing of Sanctuary and Holy Shield (Protection); Seal of Command, Sanctity Aura and Repentance (Retribution). They go in `talentSpells`, so none counts as part of a rotation until its talent is active. Consecration is a Holy talent in this data, not a trainer spell.
3. **Every spell family is classified (plan below).** An unlisted spell falls through to "keep", so silence would hide a gap. `Paladin.lua` therefore also carries a `reviewed` list for passives, effect spells and skill entries that are deliberately left at keep. A test walks the generated Paladin spell table and fails if any family is in no rotation, tag, override or `reviewed` list.
4. **Rune and engraving abilities are out.** Classic Era's data includes Season of Discovery rune abilities (Crusader Strike, Divine Storm, Beacon of Light, Avenger's Shield, Hand of Reckoning, Divine Steed and the `Engrave ...` spells). They are not sold by the class trainer, so the generator excludes the Runes and Engraving skill lines.
5. **No power-based skip ranks.** None are sourced for Paladin, so none are invented; the automatic "next rank within 4 levels" rule still applies. The class advice about skipping high-rank Auras waits for a source.
6. **Spell tables.** Generalise `build-spell-data.py` to build several classes (Paladin: mask 2; skill lines Holy, Protection, Retribution, Plate Mail, Mounts and Riding) from the Forever build, as Hunter already is, with `--keep HUNTER` so the committed Hunter prices survive. Rows named `Judgement of ...` are `kind = "effect"` so they never count as purchases. Classic Era-only families (for example Blessing of Salvation) are classified in the rule data and named in the coverage test rather than added to the table, which would make a Forever client count them as unlearned purchases. No Paladin trainer export exists, so `cost` stays unset and `costByLevel_class` borrows Hunter's table as an UNVERIFIED proxy.
7. **Milestones per class.** `Milestones.For(class)` merges `milestones` from the class data over the defaults; `Options.MilestoneItems`, `MilestoneProgress` and the status text take the class from the adapter. Paladin: level 40 `{ riding = 0, mount = 10 * 10000 }` as a placeholder (sources put the spell at about 1 to 10 gold) with the joke as a `note`; level 60 stays at the default. Forever lists Journeyman Riding as a Paladin ability and Classic Era does not, which hints that the Charger may teach it there, but that is unconfirmed, so the level 60 target is not changed.
8. **Detection.** The level 40 milestone completes when Apprentice Riding is known. The Warhorse is reported to teach it with the spell; if a client does not, the manual override exists.
9. **The joke.** The level 40 note reads along the lines of "The Light covers your first ride. The Charger sends the bill later."

## Spell plan

Levels are first-rank levels from the data; names are as the game lists them. Ranks of a family share its tier. Forever-only spells are marked (F).

**Keep, every spec (core):** Seal of Righteousness (1), Judgement (4), Holy Light (1), Flash of Light (20), Blessing of Might (4), Blessing of Wisdom (14, mana), Devotion Aura (1), Redemption (12), Purify (8), Cleanse (42), Divine Protection (6), Divine Shield (34), Summon Warhorse (40), Summon Charger (60), Plate Mail (40; kept until its price is seen).

**Defer-low-value (PvP or little use):** Blessing of Freedom (18), Blessing of Protection (10), Seal of Justice (22), Repentance (talent), Turn Undead (24), Sense Undead (12), Revoke (F, 26).

**Defer (group, raid or endgame):** Lay on Hands (10), Blessing of Sacrifice (46), Blessing of Salvation (26) and Salvation (F, 26), Mass Salvation (F, 60), Blessing of Light (40), Concentration Aura (22), Frost, Fire and Shadow Resistance Auras (32, 36, 28), Divine Intervention (30), and every Greater Blessing (Might and Wisdom from 52 and 54; Light, Kings, Sanctuary, Salvation at 60).

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **Judgement calls:** the tiers above are my classification from the class brief and general Classic knowledge, so each rotation is flagged needs-review. The Rules page already labels that.
- **Mount spells in Classic Era:** Summon Warhorse and Summon Charger exist as spells in the Era data but not as Paladin class-ability rows, so only Forever lists them under Mounts. They stay at the keep default either way.
- **Unknown trainer stock:** the tables list what a Paladin can have, not what the trainer sells. The Forever-only rows are the least certain, and the "Keep" default is the safe failure.
- **Unverified numbers:** the 10 gold target, the proxy cost table and Plate Mail's price; sources conflict on the Warhorse cost.
- **Script change:** generalising the build script risks changing the Hunter output; the Hunter table must come out unchanged.
- **Coverage test is strict:** a game build that adds a Paladin spell will fail it until the spell is classified. That is the intent.
