## Context

`Options.ClassSummary` builds the Rules page lists from `overrides` (kept), `tags` (wait, low) and `skipRanks`. It never reads `rotation.core` or `rotation.situational`, which is where every class added in v0.7.0 and v0.8.0 keeps its core spells, so Worth buying is empty for them and Optional does not exist. `skipRanks` for the new classes holds only four data-proven entries. The class rule files were generated by scratch scripts that are not in the repository.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** every spell visible; Worth buying and Optional by spec; leveling-based tiers; rank skips taken from expert guides; the level-gap feature removed; reproducible tooling.

**Non-Goals:** prices confirmed at a real trainer for the other eight classes (they use Wowhead's list until `/ddt export` captures them), new warnings, item rules.

## Decisions

1. **Summary groups by spec.** `ClassSummary` keeps `keep`, `wait`, `low`, `skipped` and gains `optional` and `other`, plus `keepGroups` and `optionalGroups` (lists of `{ title, entries }`). A spell's group is its best tier across specs (core over situational): "All specs" when every spec has it at that tier, otherwise the spec names. Overrides marked keep go to Worth buying under All specs. Names only in `reviewed` go to `other` (Not a purchase). A name in two lists by data (core for one spec, situational for another) appears once, under its best tier. `keep` stays the flat union so existing callers keep working.
2. **Page layout.** Each class page shows Worth buying and Optional as groups containing one inline sub-group per spec group, then Save your gold, Not worth buying yet, Ranks to skip, and a collapsed Not a purchase. Counts are in the headings.
3. **Coverage test.** For all nine classes: every family in the bundled table is in at least one summary list; no family is in `reviewed` and classified at once.
4. **Rank skips from experts only.** Wowhead's per-spec "Spells to Buy" guides list the levels at which to train a spell (a rank whose level is absent is skipped) or give a rank limit ("up to rank 4", "only rank 1"); where several spec guides disagree a rank is skipped only if no guide recommends it. Each class plan's `skip` holds the result with a `skip_source` note; rank 1 is never skipped; a spell the guides do not discuss gets no skip. Warlock has none (Wowhead and Icy Veins give no rank advice); Paladin has Hammer of Justice 2-4 (Icy Veins: "Just Rank 1"). Hunter keeps its committed skips except Arcane Shot 7 and Serpent Sting 7, which the Wowhead guides recommend. The user's own briefs' skips (Gouge, Kick, Psychic Scream, Shield Bash) stay unless a guide contradicts them. The computed power rule and its script were dropped.
5. **Level-gap feature removed.** The engine's supersession rule, `Rules.SupersessionWindow`, `supersessionWindow` in rule data and settings, the Tuning slider, the "Skip ranks a better one replaces soon" switch, and the next-rank-level plumbing in the adapter, ledger and spell data are deleted. The "Skip ranks that are a small upgrade" switch stays.
6. **Tooling in the repo.** `tools/class_plans/<class>.py` holds the per-class plan (rotations, tiers, reasons, skips) and `tools/scaffold_class_rules.py` writes the class Lua files from it and the cache.
7. **Leveling-based plan.** Per class, core lists by spec from the guides found (spec defaults: Frost and Fire mage; Affliction/Demonology warlock; Discipline and Holy then Shadow priest; Feral druid with Balance utility; Enhancement then Elemental shaman; Combat/Assassination rogue; Arms warrior). Key moves from v0.8.0: Smite and Holy Fire core for Discipline and Holy; Scorch core for Fire and Arcane Missiles core for Arcane; Thunder Clap, Hamstring and Slam core for Arms; Entangling Roots and Barkskin core for Balance, Faerie Fire (Feral), Prowl and Dash core for Feral; weapon imbues, shocks and Stormstrike core for Enhancement and Elemental; Gouge, Sap, Evasion, Sprint, Kick, Vanish and the Instant and Deadly Poisons core for every rogue spec; Curse of Agony, Immolate, Drain Life, Health Funnel and Searing Pain core for warlocks. The brief's "skip entirely" spells remain low value only where no spec uses them (for example Dampen Magic, Curse of Weakness, Stoneskin Totem).

8. **Audit outcomes.** After review, these were decided: Scorch, Pyroblast, Blast Wave, Holy Fire, Shackle Undead, Fade, Distract, Thunder Clap and Slam stay low value as in the original brief; Smite (Discipline and Holy), Arcane Missiles (Arcane), Entangling Roots (Balance) and Demoralizing Shout (Protection core, optional otherwise) stay as spec core; Forever-only or non-ordinary spells (acquire method 3 in the game data, plus a short named list) are Optional until confirmed; resurrections and interrupts are always core (Druid Revive and Rebirth, Mage Counterspell, Warrior Pummel and Shield Bash, Priest Silence, Paladin Rebuke, Druid Skull Bash, Warlock Spell Lock in the grimoire keep list); Hunter: Aspect of the Pack and Frost Trap are Optional. 

9. **Warlock and Paladin save gold too.** Both get a free level 40 mount and the guides say to buy liberally, but the aim stays gold saving. Their worth-buying lists are the Icy Veins "Recommended Skills to Upgrade" lists plus what the Wowhead spec guides name; spells no guide mentions are low value. Devotion Aura is Optional because Icy Veins says it can be skipped to save gold. Hammer of Justice skips ranks 2 to 4 ("Just Rank 1"). Grimoires: every Imp and Voidwalker grimoire, the Succubus's Lash of Pain, Seduction and Lesser Invisibility, and the Felhunter's Spell Lock stay unflagged; Soothing Kiss, Devour Magic, Paranoia and Tainted Blood are flagged.

10. **Icy Veins joins Wowhead for the other six classes.** A rank is skipped only if no expert page buys it. Icy Veins re-enabled Heroic Strike 5-8, Hamstring 2-3 (Warrior), Backstab 8 (Rogue) and Greater Heal 5 (Priest), and added Frostbolt 11, Conjure Food 7 (Mage), Power Word: Shield 10, Inner Fire 6 (Priest), Rockbiter Weapon 5-7 and Strength of Earth Totem 5 (Shaman). Not buying a rank counts as skipping it, including level 60 ranks. Rogue Kick 2-4 stays skipped although Icy Veins calls it optional. Spells the guides call optional or "if you have the money" stay low value, and the plans' tiers for Scorch, Holy Fire, Feint, Distract, Wound Poison, Hibernate, Thorns and the like are unchanged.

11. **Every list is grouped; Pet and built-in skills.** Rule data gains optional `general` and `pet` lists. Skills that come with the class and are never trained (Auto Shot) are left off the Rules page only; their verdicts do not change. Pet skills, the abilities a Pet Trainer teaches a Hunter's pet (Growl, Great Stamina, Natural Armor and the resistances), sit in a Pet group that comes last in whichever list they are in. Save your gold and Not worth buying yet group by tag reason, Ranks to skip by spec, Not a purchase into Pet and Other.

12. **Projected savings and trainer prices.** `Model/Savings.lua` walks a class's bundled spell rows up to the stop level, asks the rule engine about each (a talent's first rank is free, so it is skipped) and adds up the price of every rank that is deferred. Progress shows it for the character with its settings; Advanced > Classes shows, per class, each spec's total with every rule on, whatever the player has switched off, to level 60. Prices come from `Data/TrainerPrices.lua`, written by `tools/fetch-trainer-prices.py` from Wowhead's Classic spell lists (they matched all 106 Hunter prices seen at a real trainer); prices seen at the player's trainers take precedence and a level estimate fills the gaps (talent ranks, level 60 ranks Wowhead doesn't list). The Tally's "Kept so far" now also uses the listed prices.

13. **One spacing rule for every page.** The page builder owns spacing: `gap()` is a blank line as tall as body text, `section()` adds one above its heading unless a blank line is already there, and amounts use one bulleted figure format (the Tally included). The projected savings and class overview no longer place their own blank lines.

14. **Profession settings in one place, a flatter Advanced.** The profession rank switch and margin move from Tuning and the Rules tree to the Professions area of What to watch. The two read-only Herbalism and Skinning pages go. The rule kind switches (skipped ranks, off-spec leaning) move into Tuning, so Advanced is Sound & messages, Tuning, Classes (one tree) and Debug.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **Guides are uneven.** Some give exact level lists, some only narrate, and a few skips come from mechanical omissions (a guide stopping at level 58 leaves a level 60 rank unlisted) or soft wording ("usually"); each is shown on the page and can be switched off.
- **Judgement at scale.** About 600 families are assessed from leveling-guide defaults and my reading; each rotation stays flagged needs-review.
- **Guides disagree and some are for other versions.** The sources found give spec choices and a few key spells, not full lists, so spells they do not mention rely on judgement.
- **Page size.** Nine classes with six lists and spec sub-groups make a long page; the collapsed lists and counts keep it scannable.

## Migration Plan

No saved-settings changes (per-ability and per-tag off switches keep their keys). Version 0.8.1 becomes 0.9.0 at archive.
